# Personal AI Employee Summary

Personal AI employees are autonomous digital assistants that operate 24/7 to handle routine tasks, manage information, and coordinate workflows on behalf of individual users. Unlike traditional chatbots, they maintain persistent state in local knowledge bases (like Obsidian vaults), perceive events through watchers, and execute multi-step workflows with human oversight for sensitive actions. They represent a shift from on-demand AI tools to always-running digital teammates that proactively manage inbox processing, task triage, content creation, and external communications through approval-based workflows.
